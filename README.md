# Supporting Communities in Need

Free tools that help people in underserved communities understand money and run small businesses. Built first for least developed countries, and built to work offline and on paper.

## Why this exists

I was part of the team that launched Quinnipiac's micro-finance venture in Nicaragua. It was a pivotal experience for me. One lesson stayed with me: many of the small business owners we worked with treated the money coming in as money in their pocket, when it was revenue that still had to cover expenses. A tool that makes that difference visible can change a decision, and a good decision can change a business.

These projects are my way of building on that lesson. Each one is meant to be used right away by someone who needs it.

## Ideas coming next

- **Three Jars:** a simple weekly money sheet that helps a small business owner split sales into restocking, running costs, and her own pay.
- **Trip cost meter:** a way to see what a buying trip really costs, including the days the stall stays closed, plus a shared buying list so one person can shop for several stalls.
- **Loan conditions explorer:** plain-language summaries of the conditions attached to IMF programs, linked to the original documents.

## How every project is built

- It works without internet after the first visit.
- It needs no account and collects no data.
- It has a paper version, so people without a smartphone can use it too.
- It uses plain words and is translated into the languages of the people who use it.
- It is free to copy, change, and share for community use. It is not for sale.

## Work with me

I'm looking for organizations that care about real community impact to test these tools with the people they serve. Savings groups and nonprofits are a great fit. If that sounds like you, open an issue on this repository or reach out at tshugglercommunityai@gmail.com.

Translations, corrections, and feedback from the field help the most. Each project has its own folder with a guide.

If you would like to use a tool in a paid program or a commercial product, please ask me first. I am glad to talk about it.

## Please note

These tools give estimates and general information. They don't replace advice from a trusted professional or a local expert.
